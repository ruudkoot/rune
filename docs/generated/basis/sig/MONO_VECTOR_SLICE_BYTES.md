The slice, as the library sees it: the members of the specification and
the two conversions to and from a substring. [`MONO_VECTOR_BYTES`](../sig/MONO_VECTOR_BYTES.md), the
vector's, is in mono\_sigs.sml, where [`Substring`](../sig/SUBSTRING.md) is not yet in scope.
