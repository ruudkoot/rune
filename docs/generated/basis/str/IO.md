## Notes

### Io

> **Reading** `IO.Io/function-is-unqualified`. "The name of the function
> raising the exception" is taken unqualified: `"openIn"`, not
> `"TextIO.openIn"`, as MLton and SML/NJ take it. Poly/ML writes the
> qualified name.
