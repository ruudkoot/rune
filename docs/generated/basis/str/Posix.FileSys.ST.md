What stat reports. The kind is posix\_stat's: 0 regular file, 1
directory, 2 symbolic link, 4 FIFO, 5 socket, 6 character device, 7
block device, and 3 anything else (vm/sys\_posix.c, kind\_of).

## Notes

### ST.atime

> **Implementation** `Posix.FileSys.ST.atime/whole-seconds`. The three
> times are kept in whole seconds, so they have no fraction.

### ST.ctime

> **Reading** `Posix.FileSys.ST.ctime/utime-sets-it-to-now`. [`utime`](../sig/POSIX_FILE_SYS.md#val-utime)
> changes this time to now, not to either of the times it is given: it
> is the record that changed, not the contents.
