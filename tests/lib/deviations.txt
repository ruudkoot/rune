# Known failures of the libraries beside the basis library on the other
# compilers (tests/lib/run-hosts.sh), in the form of tests/basis/deviations.txt:
#
#   config-glob | label-glob | CATEGORY | reason
#
# config-glob matches native:HOST (native:mlton, native:smlnj-legacy,
# native:smlnj32, native:smlnj-dev, native:polyml, native:mlkit), label-glob
# the test's label. A line that matches no failure of a host that ran fails
# the run (not a HOST-FLAKY one): remove a line when its cause is fixed.
# A library is never changed to work around a host's bug (AGENTS.md): the
# failure is a HOST-BUG line here, with a report in docs/bugreport.

# ---- SML/NJ 110.99.9 built for 32 bits: a 64-bit literal loses bits 30 and
# 31 of its low half (docs/bugreport/smlnj/Word64-low-half), and the 64-bit
# arithmetic, shifts and conversions have the bugs of docs/bugreport/smlnj.
native:smlnj32 | property.* | HOST-BUG | lib/test/property is written on lib/random (see random.*); with lib/random right (a native fixed point of 110.99.9), property.core still fails on other bugs of docs/bugreport/smlnj (Word64, Int64 and reals), and with all their fixes it passes
native:smlnj32 | random.* | HOST-BUG | SplitMix64's 64-bit constants lose bits 30 and 31 of their low half (0wx40000000 : Word64.word is 0w0): the release's boot files are cross-compiled on amd64, which builds IntInf constants with 62-bit digits; smlnj/legacy #260, closed as fixed in 110.99.4, is still there in 110.99.9, and a native fixed point of 110.99.9 passes

# the range (tests/basis/deviations.txt, Real.fromManExp/minPos), and
# Real.nextAfter steps from the least normal real to 0.0.
native:smlnj-legacy | property.core | HOST-BUG | Real.fromManExp {man = 0.5, exp = ~1021} is 0.0, not the least normal real, and so is every subnormal, and Real.nextAfter (Real.minNormalPos, 0.0) is 0.0: lib/test/property decodes a real node's bits with fromManExp and draws that nextAfter as an edge case, so the fingerprint differs (docs/bugreport/smlnj/Real.fromManExp, Real.nextAfter); the 64-bit build also now and then loses a real in a garbage collection, as functions share GC code that does not save it (docs/bugreport/smlnj/GC/real-corrupted-on-64-bit), or stops with "bogus fault" when the collector meets a raw Word64 in a record of arguments (docs/bugreport/smlnj/GC/spilled-word64-argument)
native:smlnj-legacy | property.shrink | HOST-FLAKY | the 64-bit build now and then loses a real or a Word64 in a garbage collection (docs/bugreport/smlnj/GC/real-corrupted-on-64-bit, spilled-word64-argument): functions/pure-has-no-effects failed in one of three runs of the same program, which passes on every other compiler and on the development line
native:smlnj-dev | property.core | HOST-BUG | Real.fromManExp {man = 0.5, exp = ~1021} is 0.0, not the least normal real, and so is every subnormal, and Real.nextAfter (Real.minNormalPos, 0.0) is 0.0: lib/test/property decodes a real node's bits with fromManExp and draws that nextAfter as an edge case, so the fingerprint differs (docs/bugreport/smlnj/Real.fromManExp, Real.nextAfter)
native:smlnj-dev | property.shrink | HOST-BUG | the host stops with "Fatal error -- bogus fault not in ML" (signal 11) in planted/sort-drops-equals, in every run, and the tests after it do not run; not reduced, and perhaps the raw Word64 among the pointers of a record of arguments that 2026.3 fixed (smlnj/smlnj#394; docs/bugreport/smlnj/GC/spilled-word64-argument)
