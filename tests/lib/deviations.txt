# Known failures of the libraries beside the basis library on the other
# compilers (tests/lib/run-hosts.sh), in the form of tests/basis/deviations.txt:
#
#   config-glob | label-glob | CATEGORY | reason
#
# config-glob matches native:HOST (native:mlton, native:smlnj,
# native:smlnj32, native:polyml, native:mlkit), label-glob the test's label.
# A library is never changed to work around a host's bug (AGENTS.md): the
# failure is a HOST-BUG line here, with a report in docs/bugreport.

# ---- SML/NJ 110.99.9 built for 32 bits: a 64-bit literal loses bits 30 and
# 31 of its low half (docs/bugreport/smlnj/Word64-low-half), and the 64-bit
# arithmetic, shifts and conversions have the bugs of docs/bugreport/smlnj.
native:smlnj32 | property.* | HOST-BUG | lib/test/property is written on lib/random (see random.*); with lib/random right (a native fixed point of 110.99.9), property.core still fails on other bugs of docs/bugreport/smlnj (Word64, Int64 and reals), and with all their fixes it passes
native:smlnj32 | random.* | HOST-BUG | SplitMix64's 64-bit constants lose bits 30 and 31 of their low half (0wx40000000 : Word64.word is 0w0): the release's boot files are cross-compiled on amd64, which builds IntInf constants with 62-bit digits; smlnj/legacy #260, closed as fixed in 110.99.4, is still there in 110.99.9, and a native fixed point of 110.99.9 passes
