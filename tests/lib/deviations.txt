# Known failures of the libraries beside the basis library on the other
# compilers (tests/lib/run-hosts.sh), in the form of tests/basis/deviations.txt:
#
#   config-glob | label-glob | CATEGORY | reason
#
# config-glob matches native:HOST (native:mlton, native:smlnj,
# native:smlnj32, native:polyml, native:mlkit), label-glob the test's label.
# A library is never changed to work around a host's bug (AGENTS.md): the
# failure is a HOST-BUG line here, with a report in docs/bugreport.

# ---- SML/NJ built for 32 bits: a Word64.word loses bits 30 and 31 of its
# low half, in literals and in arithmetic (docs/bugreport/smlnj/Word64-low-half).
native:smlnj32 | property.* | HOST-BUG | lib/test/property is written on lib/random: see random.*
native:smlnj32 | random.* | HOST-BUG | Word64 values lose bits 30 and 31 of their low half (0wx40000000 : Word64.word is 0w0), so SplitMix64's constants and sums are wrong; smlnj/legacy #260, closed as fixed in 110.99.4, is still there in 110.99.9

# ---- SML/NJ 110.99.9 (both builds): Real.fromManExp is 0.0 at the bottom of
# the range, which tests/basis/deviations.txt records as Real.fromManExp/minPos.
native:smlnj | property.core | HOST-BUG | Real.fromManExp {man = 0.5, exp = ~1021} is 0.0, not the least normal real, and so is every subnormal: lib/test/property decodes a real node's bits with it, so those reals are 0.0 and the fingerprint differs (Real.fromManExp/minPos in tests/basis/deviations.txt)
