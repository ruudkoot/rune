# The laws of the Basis Library's documentation that do not hold in Rune, or
# are not tested there (docs/plans/quickcheck.md, D12, as the owner changed it
# on 2026-09-28; docs/plans/quickcheck-hunt.md):
#
#   label-glob | FAILS or UNTESTED | why
#
# The glob matches a law's label at a structure, SIGNATURE.member/law-N@S, with
# * ? and [...] as the shell has them. FAILS: the law does not hold. UNTESTED:
# it is not run, because the tester gives up on it or it is no Standard ML at
# the structure. `runedoc --law-skips` marks every law listed here on its page,
# with the reason, and fails on a line that names no law.
# make check (tests/runner/laws.sml) counts a listed law's failure as known, and a listed
# law that passes as a line to remove.

# ---- Real arithmetic: laws that hold over the reals and not in floating point
# (a general solution is for later: docs/plans/quickcheck.md, Later)
MATH.atan2/law-1@* | FAILS | it is a law of the reals. In floating point `y / x` is rounded before `atan` sees it, so the two sides can differ in the last place, and for two infinities `y / x` is a NaN; `atan2` is the side that is correctly rounded (the counterexample).

# ---- Laws for `char` that are no Standard ML at the wide structures. `STRING`
# and `SUBSTRING` name no structure of their characters, so a law that needs
# one names `Char` or `String`, and holds only where those are the characters
# (companion structures, for later: docs/plans/quickcheck.md, Later).
STRING.compare/law-1@WideString | UNTESTED | it names `Char.compare`, and the characters of `WideString` are `WideChar.char`.
STRING.toString/law-1@WideString | UNTESTED | it names `Char.toString`, and the characters of `WideString` are `WideChar.char`.
STRING.fromString/law-1@WideString | UNTESTED | `StringCvt.scanString` gives `scan` the characters of a `String.string`, and `WideString.scan` reads those of `WideChar.char`.
SUBSTRING.compare/law-1@WideSubstring | UNTESTED | it names `String.compare`, and the strings of `WideSubstring` are `WideString.string`.
STRING_CVT.skipWS/law-1@WideStringCvt | UNTESTED | it names `Char.isSpace`, and the characters of `WideStringCvt` are `WideChar.char`.

# ---- The laws of the streams at WideTextIO, which matches TEXT_IO since the
# deviation TEXT_IO/generic: lib/test/property/basis has the arbitraries of
# the streams of TextIO (system.sml) and none yet of WideTextIO's.
IMPERATIVE_IO.*@WideTextIO | UNTESTED | lib/test/property/basis has no arbitrary of a stream of `WideTextIO` yet; those of `TextIO` are in its system.sml.
STREAM_IO.*@WideTextIO.StreamIO | UNTESTED | lib/test/property/basis has no arbitrary of a stream of `WideTextIO.StreamIO` yet; those of `TextIO.StreamIO` are in its system.sml.

# ---- Laws the tester gives up on: their condition is seldom met (variables
# drawn together, for later: docs/plans/quickcheck.md, Later)
LIST_PAIR.unzip/law-1@ListPair | UNTESTED | the tester seldom draws two lists of one length, and gives up; `zip (unzip l) = l`, the law turned round, is tested.
