# What the other compilers do differently from Rune with the laws of the Basis
# Library's documentation (tests/basis/run-laws-hosts.py; docs/plans/quickcheck.md,
# D10; docs/plans/quickcheck-hunt.md), in the form of tests/basis/deviations.txt:
#
#   config-glob | label-glob | CATEGORY | reason
#
# config-glob matches native:HOST, label-glob a law's label at a structure. A
# difference from Rune that no line explains is the hunt's to classify: the law,
# Rune, or the host. Rune is never changed to agree with a host, and a host's bug
# is a line here (AGENTS.md); the probe that shows it is in the reason.

# ---- Poly/ML 5.9.2
native:polyml | ARRAY2.nRows/law-1@Array2 | HOST-BUG | Array2.dimensions of an empty array raises Subscript (dimensions (Array2.fromList []) should be (0, 0)), so nRows arr = #1 (dimensions arr) fails at it
native:polyml | MONO_ARRAY2.nRows/law-1@* | HOST-BUG | dimensions of an empty array raises Subscript, as Array2's does
native:polyml | INT_INF.~>>/law-1@IntInf | HOST-BUG | IntInf.~>> rounds a negative number of more than 62 bits towards zero: ~>> (~4611686018427387905, 0w1) is ~2305843009213693952, where the floor is ~2305843009213693953
native:polyml | OS_PATH.splitDirFile/law-1@OS.Path | SPEC-AMBIGUOUS | joinDirFile raises InvalidArc for an arc that is NUL, as SML/NJ does; Rune, MLton and MLKit take it. The specification leaves which arcs are valid to the system, and POSIX names hold no NUL

# ---- MLKit 4.7.23
native:mlkit | INT_INF.~>>/law-1@IntInf | HOST-BUG | IntInf.~>> rounds towards zero: ~>> (~1, 0w1) is 0, not ~1, and so for the negative numbers of more than 62 bits
native:mlkit | INTEGER.toInt/law-1@IntInf | HOST-BUG | IntInf.toInt raises Overflow at 2^31, although int has 63 bits
native:mlkit | INTEGER.toInt/law-1@LargeInt | HOST-BUG | LargeInt is IntInf, whose toInt raises Overflow at 2^31, although int has 63 bits
native:mlkit | SOCKET.SOCK.fromString/law-1@Socket | HOST-BUG | Socket.SOCK.toString of the raw type is "RAM", which fromString does not read back
native:mlkit | POSIX_SYS_DB.getgrgid/law-1@Posix.SysDB | HOST-BUG | Posix.SysDB.getgrgid of the group 1000 raises Overflow
native:mlkit | POSIX_SYS_DB.getpwuid/law-1@Posix.SysDB | HOST-BUG | Posix.SysDB.getpwuid of the user 1000 raises Overflow

# ---- MLton 20241230
native:mlton | POSIX_TTY.getiflag/law-4@Posix.TTY | HOST-BUG | Posix.TTY.getlflag returns the termios's oflag: of a termios with lflag 0w0 and oflag 0w1 it is 0w1

# ---- every host
native:* | PRIM_IO.compare/law-1@TextPrimIO | HOST-ABSENT | the arbitrary of positions takes them from the reader of TextPrimIO.openVector, which has no getPos on the other compilers (the specification makes it optional), so the law cannot be made there

# ---- Date.toTime of a date at UTC
native:mlton | DATE.fromTimeUniv/law-1@Date | HOST-BUG | Date.toTime (Date.fromTimeUniv Time.zeroTime) raises Date, where Poly/ML and Rune give the epoch
native:smlnj* | DATE.fromTimeUniv/law-1@Date | HOST-BUG | Date.toTime (Date.fromTimeUniv Time.zeroTime) raises Date, where Poly/ML and Rune give the epoch
native:mlkit | DATE.fromTimeUniv/law-1@Date | HOST-BUG | Date.toTime (Date.fromTimeUniv Time.zeroTime) raises Date, where Poly/ML and Rune give the epoch

# ---- SML/NJ 110.99.9 (both builds) and 2026.2
native:smlnj* | GENERAL.exnMessage/law-1@General | HOST-BUG | exnMessage Overflow is "overflow" and exnMessage Div "divide by zero", which do not hold exnName ex, as the specification asks
native:smlnj* | OS_PATH.splitBaseExt/law-1@OS.Path | HOST-BUG | splitBaseExt "//" has the base "/", and joinBaseExt of it is "/": a separator is lost
native:smlnj* | OS_PATH.splitDirFile/law-1@OS.Path | SPEC-AMBIGUOUS | joinDirFile raises InvalidArc for an arc that is NUL, as Poly/ML does; Rune, MLton and MLKit take it. The specification leaves which arcs are valid to the system, and POSIX names hold no NUL
native:smlnj* | STRING_CVT.skipWS/law-1@StringCvt | HOST-BUG | StringCvt.skipWS does not skip the vertical tab, which Char.isSpace counts as space
native:smlnj* | STREAM_IO.input/law-1@* | HOST-BUG | TextIO.StreamIO.inputAll of TextIO.openString "" raises Io, from Div; the arbitrary of instreams shows one by reading it all, so the law cannot be made
native:smlnj* | STREAM_IO.input1/law-1@* | HOST-BUG | TextIO.StreamIO.inputAll of TextIO.openString "" raises Io, from Div; the arbitrary of instreams shows one by reading it all, so the law cannot be made

# ---- SML/NJ 110.99.9, 64 bits: a garbage collection now and then corrupts a
# Word64 or a real (docs/bugreport/smlnj/GC/real-corrupted-on-64-bit,
# spilled-word64-argument); a drawn function then gives two outcomes for one
# argument, or a real changes
native:smlnj-legacy | ARRAY_SLICE.foldl/law-1@* | HOST-FLAKY | a drawn function gave two outcomes for one argument: the 64-bit build's garbage collection corrupts a Word64 now and then
native:smlnj-legacy | STRING_CVT.takel/law-1@* | HOST-FLAKY | a drawn function gave two outcomes for one argument: the 64-bit build's garbage collection corrupts a Word64 now and then
native:smlnj-legacy | MONO_VECTOR.fromList/law-1@Real64Vector | HOST-FLAKY | a real changed between two reads (both print 8.078194267465491E37): the 64-bit build's garbage collection corrupts a real now and then

# ---- Date for years outside 1900 to 2200, where an implementation may raise
# Date: the laws hold there when a date keeps its fields or Date is raised
native:mlton | DATE.year/law-*@Date | HOST-BUG | Date.date raises Overflow for a year it cannot represent, where the specification asks for Date
native:mlkit | DATE.year/law-*@Date | HOST-BUG | Date.date raises Overflow for a year it cannot represent, where the specification asks for Date
