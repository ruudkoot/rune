# What the other compilers do differently from Rune with the laws of the Basis
# Library's documentation (tests/basis/run-laws-hosts.py; docs/plans/quickcheck.md,
# D10; docs/plans/quickcheck-hunt.md), in the form of tests/basis/deviations.txt:
#
#   config-glob | label-glob | CATEGORY | reason
#
# config-glob matches native:HOST, label-glob a law's label at a structure. A
# difference from Rune that no line explains is the hunt's to classify: the law,
# Rune, or the host. Rune is never changed to agree with a host, and a host's bug
# is a line here (AGENTS.md); the probe that shows it is in the reason.

# ---- Poly/ML 5.9.2
native:polyml | ARRAY2.nRows/law-1@Array2 | HOST-BUG | Array2.dimensions of an empty array raises Subscript (dimensions (Array2.fromList []) should be (0, 0)), so nRows arr = #1 (dimensions arr) fails at it (docs/bugreport/polyml/Array2.dimensions/no-rows-Subscript)
native:polyml | MONO_ARRAY2.nRows/law-1@* | HOST-BUG | dimensions of an empty array raises Subscript, as Array2's does (docs/bugreport/polyml/Array2.dimensions/no-rows-Subscript)
native:polyml | INT_INF.~>>/law-1@IntInf | HOST-BUG | IntInf.~>> rounds a negative number of more than 62 bits towards zero: ~>> (~4611686018427387905, 0w1) is ~2305843009213693952, where the floor is ~2305843009213693953 (docs/bugreport/polyml/IntInf/shift-right-rounds-towards-zero)
native:polyml | OS_PATH.splitDirFile/law-1@OS.Path | SPEC-AMBIGUOUS | joinDirFile raises InvalidArc for an arc that is NUL, as SML/NJ does; Rune, MLton and MLKit take it. The specification leaves which arcs are valid to the system, and POSIX names hold no NUL

# ---- MLKit 4.7.25
native:mlkit | INT_INF.~>>/law-1@IntInf | HOST-BUG | IntInf.~>> rounds towards zero: ~>> (~1, 0w1) is 0, not ~1, and so for the negative numbers of more than 62 bits (docs/bugreport/mlkit/IntInf/shift-right-rounds-towards-zero)
native:mlkit | SOCKET.SOCK.fromString/law-1@Socket | HOST-BUG | Socket.SOCK.toString of the raw type is "RAM", which fromString does not read back (docs/bugreport/mlkit/Socket.SOCK.toString/raw-is-RAM)
native:mlkit | POSIX_SYS_DB.getgrgid/law-1@Posix.SysDB | HOST-BUG | Posix.SysDB.getgrgid of the group 1000 raises Overflow (docs/bugreport/mlkit/Posix.SysDB.getpwuid/untagged-results)
native:mlkit | POSIX_SYS_DB.getpwuid/law-1@Posix.SysDB | HOST-BUG | Posix.SysDB.getpwuid of the user 1000 raises Overflow (docs/bugreport/mlkit/Posix.SysDB.getpwuid/untagged-results)

# ---- MLton 20241230
native:mlton | POSIX_TTY.getiflag/law-4@Posix.TTY | HOST-BUG | Posix.TTY.getlflag returns the termios's oflag: of a termios with lflag 0w0 and oflag 0w1 it is 0w1 (docs/bugreport/mlton/Posix.TTY.getlflag/returns-oflag)
