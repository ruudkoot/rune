## Translation and runtime behavior

Use the same portable kernel, binary32 arithmetic in binary64 storage,
GHC atan2 algorithm, strict maximum fold, original data/order and documented
Math replacement. The policy changes demand, sharing and allocation;
software rounding and support cells remain measured representation costs.
See the canonical translation section for every adaptation and argument rule.

## Inputs and validation

conformation/coordinate oracle. Precision, exact pruning counts, demand,
sharing and fault controls follow the canonical policy. Private observations
are separate from host/profile acceptance; registration does not close M3.
